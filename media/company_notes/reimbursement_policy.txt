REIMBURSEMENT POLICY- PIXL
Effective Date: 1
st April 2025
Purpose: The purpose of this policy is to define types of expenses covered by the Company and what is
the process of claiming and getting the payment.
Scope: This policy applies to all full-time employees and consultants of PIXL Group DMCC working in the
UAE.
1. Reimbursable Expenses
a. Mobile expenses- Employees whose roles require frequent communication with clients or
vendors will be provided with a corporate calling plan to cover their expenses. If the cost exceeds
the plan limit, the employee must provide a justification along with supporting documentation.
Failure to do so may result in the employee bearing the additional charges.
b. Fuel and Parking Expenses: Employees whose roles require frequent meetings with clients or
vendors are eligible for this expense. Fuel expenses can be claimed at a rate of 1.5 AED per
kilometre, while parking expenses are reimbursed on actuals. The combined reimbursement for
fuel and parking is capped at AED 800 per month or the actual amount incurred, whichever is
lower. This allowance is strictly for client visits and does not cover commuting between home and
office.
c. Taxi Expenses: Employees whose roles involve frequent meetings with clients or vendors are
eligible for this expense. They can claim up to 800 AED for taxi expenses or the actual amount
incurred, whichever is lower. This allowance is strictly limited to client visits and does not cover
home to office visits.
If an employee is claiming both taxi and fuel expenses, the total reimbursement cannot exceed
the AED 800 monthly cap.
Any additional expenses related to client engagement and meetings will be considered on an
exception basis and require separate approval.
2. How to submit reimbursement claims:
a. Supporting Documents
• All claims must include the necessary supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices.
• Receipts must clearly show the issue date, itemized details of the expense, and any
relevant information required for reimbursement.
• Taxi receipts should include full pick-up and drop-off locations, along with the rides time.
• For rides on weekends, please provide a clarification in the reimbursement sheet
explaining the business purpose of the ride.
• Claims without proper documentation will not be approved.
b. Phone Bill Claims
• Submit a clear and complete copy of your monthly phone bill.
• Highlight any charges that are not covered by the company, such as international calls
for personal use or add-on services.
• This will assist the Finance team in verifying the details promptly.
3. Non reimbursable expenses:
a. Expenses claimed without any supporting documentation or approvals. We will not accept
screenshots of debit messages, telephone bill payment receipts without the actual bill, taxi
fare receipts without taxi bill, email screenshots of taxi rides.
b. Meals not directly related to business meetings or work activities are not reimbursable. Client
entertainment expenses require prior approval. Additionally, tips exceeding 10-15% of the
meal cost will not be covered.
c. Valet parking.
d. Traffic tickets, parking fines, or any other penalties incurred during business activities.
e. Alcoholic beverages unless specifically permitted under certain circumstances (e.g., client
entertainment with prior approval).
f. Gifts for personal reasons that are not part of a company-approved business expense.
g. Any tools, software, or services purchased without prior approval from HR or higher
management.
4. Submission Guidelines
All reimbursement claims must be submitted to Finance by the 21st of the following month. If the
21st falls on a non-working day, the submission deadline moves to the previous working day.
For example:
Expenses incurred between January 1st and January 31st must be submitted to your immediate
supervisor or department head latest by February 15th and to Finance latest by February 21st
with all necessary approvals.
Similarly, expenses from February 1st onward should be submitted to your supervisor by March
15th and to Finance by March 21st with approvals in place.
a. Key Guidelines:
i. Submission to Supervisor: Claims must be submitted to your supervisor no later than
the 15th of each month.
ii. Approval & Forwarding: Approved claims must be forwarded to Finance no later than
the 21st of each month.
iii. Strict Deadlines: Claims submitted after the 21st will not be considered. Likewise, claims
submitted before the 10th of each month will also not be processed. This timeline ensures
adequate time for review and consolidation before payroll processing.
iv. Employee Responsibility: It is the employee’s sole responsibility to ensure that
approved claims are submitted to Finance within the specified timelines.
v. If for any reason supervisor has failed to approve on time after follow ups, employee
should address the concern to HR.
b. Approval Process: The respective employee should prepare the reimbursement sheet and
submit it to their department head for approval. The approved email, along with all relevant
attachments, should then be forwarded to the finance team. Employees are expected to share
details of document submitted for ease of verification and consideration.
c. Payment Process: Once verified by the finance team, the reimbursement will be processed and
included with the employee's salary.
d. Responsibility: Employees are responsible for ensuring they are providing proper documentation
to support reimbursement claims and finance team receives the approved claims on or before the
21st of each month. Respective managers are responsible to check the details before approving
claims. Finance team is responsible to verify the data and ask for clarifications before initiating
the payment.
e. Late Submissions: Claims submitted beyond deadline will not be processed unless there are
exceptional circumstances, subject to senior management approval.
f. Payment: Expenses will only be reimbursed if they are:
• Submitted on mail to finance team with the approval of their managers.
• Supported by relevant documents, where available, for example, receipts, tickets and digital
copy. Credit or debit card slips will not be accepted.
• Where required, authorised approval at the time the expense was incurred.
5. Exceptions
The Company will reimburse expenses incurred by the Employee in accordance with this policy.
Any exceptions must receive prior approval from the HOD and CEO before being submitted for
processing.
Any attempt to claim expenses in violation of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
The Company reserves the right to change the policy at any time, for whatever reason, without
any prior notification. If the company deems it appropriate to change this policy, the Company
will issue an update.
