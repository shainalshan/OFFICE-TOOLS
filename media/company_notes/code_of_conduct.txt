I. COVERAGE
All PIXL talents/employees regardless of rank, status, tenure, or nationality shall be covered by this policy.
II. GENERAL STATEMENT POLICY
The management of PIXL recognizes that rules, regulations and disciplinary measures are necessary to ensure
orderly and successful operation of the business. These are also crucial in safeguarding the rights, safety, and general
welfare of all organization members.
The company believes that such regulations and measures should be developed and administered in a fair,
reasonable and legal manner.
For these reasons the company has developed its Rules and Regulations and the Code of Conduct to promote the
standards of acceptable conduct and to guide management action in event of violations. To enable the company to
withstand competition, sustain its profitability and growth, and develop a corporate culture of excellence,
compliance with these regulations and respect for the Code of Discipline is imperative.
III. GUIDELINES AND PROCEDURE
A. Specific Provisions - General Company Rules and Regulations
1. Relating to Customers/Clients/Other employees
1.1 All talents/employees, trainees, part-timers should equally respect and properly accommodate all
customers/clients regardless of age, race, sex, religion, social status or appearance.
1.2 All talents/employees should disclose known information to PIXL related to working with clients if they are a
relative of an employee.
1.3 No talents/employees shall be hired if they are the spouse/relative of an employee already employed by PIXL.
2. Safeguarding the Company’s Image
2.1 The success and image of PIXL depends significantly on the maintenance and upliftment of standards. The
principle behind PIXL’s excellence in quality and service – applies to everyone in PIXL.
2.2 It is every talent’s duty and responsibility to uphold and safeguard these standards and protect the Company’s
image at all times and in all aspects of work.
2.3 Dress code: All talents/employees should always be neat and tidy in appearance, and well-groomed so as to
give a good and professional image of PIXL to clients and visitors as well as to promote and maintain a good
working environment in the office. Further dress code policies will be department based.
3. Handling Company Property
Talents/employees are enjoined:
3.1 To exercise utmost caution and care in handling and using all company equipment, furniture, tools, facilities and
materials, and PIXL provided devices.
3.2 To handle and use only the company property which they are authorized to use and handle.
4. Safety and Security
4.1 No talent/employee may divulge, pass on or keep for personal use any confidential knowledge or document
considered as company property (e.g. trade secrets, contracts, etc.)
4.2 No talent/employee may divulge their personal information in their offer letter/contract regarding remuneration
or commission to other members of PIXL.
5. Accounts / Finance Matters
The finance department of the company has laid down company policies and procedures covering salary, cash
advances, check preparations, reimbursements and other money matters. Everyone is expected to know and abide by
these policies.

7. Observing Work Hours and Annual Leave
7.1 All talents/employees are to follow their assigned work schedules. Any change in work schedule should be with
authorization from a talent's manager.
7.2 In case a talent/employee cannot report for work, he/she shall personally notify his/her manager at least 2 hours
before his scheduled work. For illness/sick/injury leave an email should be sent to the manager with cc to HR.
Should your sickness, illness and/or injury exceed three (3) consecutive days, you are required to provide a duly
certified doctor’s certificate.
The talent/employee, following successful completion of his/ her probation period shall (unless such absence results
from the employee's misbehaviour) be entitled to absence due to incapacity/ illness up to an aggregate of ninety (90)
calendar days in any twelve (12) month period as follows:
A. The first fifteen (15) calendar days, with full pay;
B. The next thirty (30) calendar days, with half pay; and
C. Any subsequent period, without pay.
7.3 Annual leaves entitlement: thirty (30) calendar days per employee/talent during a period of 1 January - 31
December, can only be availed once the probation period is completed. In calculating the duration of the annual
leave, the holidays established by law or by agreement shall not be included if they fall within the annual leave
enjoyed by the employee/talent and shall not be considered part thereof.
7.3.1 The talents/employee’s annual leave entitlement is as follows:
a) After six (6) months probation, the talent/employee shall be entitled to thirty (30) calendar days' paid leave
per annum to be accrued pro rata after he/ she has completed his/ her six (6) months of service.
b) Upon termination of the employee’s service, the talent/employee shall receive an amount in lieu of the
annual leave accrued but not taken calculated on the basis of the employee’s basic salary.
7.3.2 The talent/employee may carry forward up to ten (10) working days of accrued but untaken annual leaves
to the next year for a maximum period of twelve (12) months, after which the unused annual leaves shall expire
unless otherwise agreed by the company.
7.3.3 In case an employee joins the organization mid-year, the days of available annual leaves will be calculated
on a pro-rata basis.
7.4 For all annual leaves, prior notice to and approval of the immediate manager is mandatory. The notice timelines
are as below:
Duration Notice to be given
1-2 Days 1 Week before Day 1 of planned leave
3-4 Days 2 Weeks before Day 1 of planned leave
5-7 Days 3 Weeks before Day 1 of planned leave
8+ Days 4 Weeks before Day 1 of planned leave
7.5 Work From Home / Remote Work
All talents/employees are entitled to avail twelve (12) days per calendar year to work from home (WFH) and/or
remote work. These twelve (12) business days cannot be carried over in a new year nor can they be availed in
succession. Requests to WFH/remote work must be approved in advance by the immediate manager with reasoning.

7.6 Maternity leave:
a) All female talents/employees are entitled to 60 days of maternity leave in accordance with the UAE Labour
Law as follows:
● The first 45 days with full pay; and
● The following 15 days with half pay.
b) The talent/employee’s other contractual entitlements and benefits shall continue during their maternity leave
and the employee shall continue to accrue annual leave during this time.
c) If a female talent/employee exhausts her paid maternity leave as mentioned under clause 7.6 (a) above, she
may take additional unpaid leave for a period not exceeding 45 days (whether continuous or intermittent) if
such leave is due to an illness that befell on her or her child resulting from pregnancy or childbirth which
renders her unable to work. Sickness for the purpose of availing the additional 45 days’ unpaid leave shall
be evidenced by a medical certificate and such period of leave shall not be counted towards the female
talent/employee’s period of employment with the company for the purpose of calculating end of service
gratuity.
d) In the event a female talent/employee gives birth to a sick or disabled child requiring a constant companion,
the female talent/employee shall be entitled to a further 30 days’ leave with full pay after the end of the
maternity leave period and she shall have the right to extend for another 30 days without pay, in accordance
with the UAE Labour Law.
e) Female talents/employees may request to go on maternity leave starting from the last day of the month
immediately preceding the expected month of delivery and this shall be proven by providing a medical
certificate. The company shall also have the discretion to approve an application to go on maternity leave
which is at an earlier date.
f) Once a female talent/employee returns from maternity leave and for a period not exceeding 6 months from
the date of delivery, a female employee shall be entitled to 1 or 2 breaks for nursing her child, provided that
the duration of both breaks combined do not exceed 1 hour.
g) If a female talent/employee works for another employer during her maternity leave and/or additional leave
as stipulated under this clause, the company shall have the right to take disciplinary action and withhold
payment due to the female employee for the maternity leave or if the female employee has been paid for the
leave, she is required to return the remuneration paid.
7.7 Parental leave: All talents/employees are entitled to parental leave in accordance with the UAE Labour Law,
which is for a period of five days, to be taken within six months of the birth of the child, either intermittently or
continuously.
7.8 Bereavement leave: All talents/employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave of 5 days, in the case of the
death of a spouse. All talents/employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave of 3 days, in the case of the
death of a mother, father, child, brother, sister, grandchild, grandfather or grandmother. Immediate manager of
the talent/employee must approve the taking and timing of any leave. All talents/employees will be required to
submit proof of the death after returning to work.
7.9 Study leave: If the talent/employee has been employed by the company for at least 2 years, the talent/employee
shall be entitled to a study leave for a period of 10 working days if he or she is studying in an educational
institute approved in the UAE and this leave may be availed by the talent/employee, only for the purpose of
sitting exams. The company may request for proof of the dates when he or she is planning on sitting the exams.
The above study leave may be availed by the talent/employee if he or she obtains an academic acceptance from
one of the accredited institutes or colleges in the UAE, indicating the type of study, specialization and duration
of the study.

7.10Sabbatical leave: If the talent/employee is a UAE national, he or she shall be entitled to paid sabbatical leave in

8. Conduct and Behavior
8.1 All talents/employees shall conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the company’s good image and basic
decorum.
8.2 Talents/employees shall never instigate and/or engage in any of the following acts reflective of undesirable
conduct and behavior.
8.2.1 Threatening or attempting to physically harm any person within company premises during work hours or
off hours. This rule also applies outside company premises.
8.2.2 Showing gross disrespect or insubordination to superiors or any person of authority through any
physical/verbal attack or refusing to obey superior’s instructions.
8.3 All talents/employees are mandated to respect each talent and stakeholders’ belief, tradition, culture and religion.
For foreign nationals, all are enjoined to carefully follow and respect the culture, tradition and religion of the locals.
B. Implementing Guidelines- Code of Discipline
1. Categories of Infractions
The following categories define the various degrees of infractions to company rules.
1.1 Category “A” – Minor Infraction
Infractions of this nature create minor disruption and do not warrant immediate penalty upon the
commission. However, excessive repetition of infractions will be penalized accordingly.
1.2 Category “B” – Serious Infraction
Forms of misconduct or offenses which cause considerable disruption within the work group and
affect the smooth flow of operations.
1.3 Category “C” – Very Serious Infraction
Offenses which show willful disregard for established rules, regulations and lawful authority.
1.4 Category “D” – Extremely Serious Infraction
Action poses an imminent or potential threat to the integrity, security, property, or welfare of the
company and/or its talents/employees: or to the interest of company business as a service entity.

2.2 Notwithstanding the aforesaid categorization, the company shall have the right to immediately terminate the
services of an employee/talent without notice in the event he or she commits an act which falls under Article 44 of
the UAE Labour Law.
3. Guidelines in Selecting Penalties
3.1 Management reserves the right to prescribe or modify the proper and just penalty for all infractions
whether specified in the code or not and in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
3.2 The proper penalty as prescribed in the code or as decided by management shall be imposed by
managers after conducting their own investigations and/or after review by HR.
4. Reckoning Period for Penalties
4.1 A written warning or violations shall remain in the talent’s file for six (6) months from the date of the
infraction, after which it shall be stricken off and no longer be basis for disciplinary action.
4.2 Any talent who commits which poses an imminent and grave threat to integrity or security of the
company or the health and well being of its talents/employees or customers/clients shall be placed under
preventive suspension in accordance with law and then terminated.
4.3 A talent who offers to resign instead of being dismissed may be allowed to do so at management’s
discretion.
5. General Rules and Regulations
5.1 All violations to company rules are to be dealt with immediately, fairly consistently, and in accordance
with procedures’ outlined in this Code of Conduct.
5.2 The responsibility to maintain discipline primarily belongs to managers. HR will provide service and
counsel to managers in all cases requiring HR mediation.
5.3 Due process shall always be followed in acting on any case of infraction of company rules. No one
should be penalized without a fair opportunity to air his side.
5.4 Any manager who violates or tolerates any violation of company rules shall be penalized accordingly.
5.5 For Code of Conduct violations against or involving company property, the talent concerned shall pay
the cost involved.
6. Table of Infractions and Penalties
6.1 The Code of Discipline outlines the suggested penalties for sample infractions to company rules.
6.2 Said table merely serves as a general guideline for managers in handling cases. In actual cases,
management’s final decision shall still be based on evaluation of all circumstances/merits of a case. This
explains why the list of infractions cited in the Code of Discipline does not cover all possible violations of
the company rules.
IV. RESPONSIBILITY
All managers shall be responsible for implementing this policy.
HR will provide guidance to managers and review and decide on all cases requiring HR intervention.
